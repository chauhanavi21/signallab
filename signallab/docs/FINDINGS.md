All policies detect both labeled incidents in these runs. The threshold policy reacts quickly but produces many non-actionable episodes. Multiwindow alerts reduce firing time and avoid the brief disturbance in these traces, while detecting much later. Seed 2026's short-window policy splits an actionable incident into multiple episodes, which is why episodes are not interchangeable with incidents.

This supports a narrow conclusion: the implementation makes the expected noise/delay/reset trade-off measurable on this constructed workload. It does not establish a statistically reliable ranking, optimal thresholds, or a reduction in real pages. Three noise seeds share the same incident timing and severity; they are not three independent real-world incident datasets. Firing minutes are not the number of notifications sent—this app sends none.
